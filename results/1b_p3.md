# 1b, the near-brane throat (P3): the paper's log made exact, as an integer shot, 2026-11-12

**First line:** this computes a geometry on paper; it does not create one. The numbers below are the shape of the fifth dimension
between Maldacena and Milekhin's fine-tuned brane and the throat where their magnetic field lines bundle (arXiv:2008.06618), in the
one-dimensional problem their eq. (3.24) stands on, solved as an integer ordinary differential equation. They are a PC build's
development runs, labelled so; the two machines run the same program in the eighth queue, and the page says which chains they must print.

## What was computed

The reviewer's specification (verified by its own adversarial rebuild with symbolic checks on global AdS2 slices, a binary128 Taylor
integrator and four Q.56 implementations) reduces the five-dimensional Einstein-Maxwell equations with an AdS2 x S2 slicing to three
equations in the proper depth rho for the two warp functions A (the AdS2 part) and sigma (the S2 part):

    (Es)  sigma'' + 2 A' sigma' + 2 sigma'^2            = 4 + kappa e^{-2 sigma} - (2/3) Q2 e^{-4 sigma}
    (EA)  A'' + 2 A'^2 + 2 A' sigma' + e^{-2A}          = 4 + (Q2/3) e^{-4 sigma}
    (H)   A'^2 + 4 A' sigma' + sigma'^2 + e^{-2A}       = 6 + kappa e^{-2 sigma} - (Q2/2) e^{-4 sigma}

with L = R5 = 1 and kappa = 1. The brane is the paper's one-sided fine-tuned brane, A' = sigma' = 1 there, and the constraint (H) ties
A to sigma on it, so one number is free: the shot ell = S^2/Q2 with S the S2 radius at the brane, which through the paper's (2.5), (3.23)
and (3.24) is exactly ln(L_IR/R5), the logarithm the paper estimated as "L_IR ~ l_B". The program (`code/wh_p3.c`, on `code/whq56.h`, the
shared fixed-point primitives at 56 fraction bits) integrates inward from the brane in the depth t = rho_b - rho the three rescaled
variables alpha = A - A_b, s = sigma - sigma_b and bh = (sigma' - 1)/Z with Z = Q2 e^{-4 sigma} (near the brane A' - 1 is 1e-23 at the
paper's charge, below any fixed-point grid, while bh stays of order ten); A' comes from (H)'s + root in the form -c/(B + sqrt(B^2 - Z c)),
which does not cancel as Z -> 0, and (EA) is never integrated inward (a constraint error would grow like e^{8t} through the AdS5 layer).
RK4 with the step 2^-12 and the once-rounded update with the carried remainder, in Q.56 (values below 128: ell runs 5.3 to 14.5 and the
RK4 sum reaches 83). The condition at the throat is 1a's: the solution must land on the AdS3 x S2 fixed point without the growing mode. In
integers that is a bisection of ell on the first event of each trial, with no tolerance and no fitted depth: sigma' < 0 means ell too large;
s below its fixed-point value, A' <= 0, or a negative radicand at a stage means ell too small. The first integral W = e^{2 alpha - 2 s}
(ah - bh) is carried against its integrated right side as a check.

## What this is and is not

It is the second of the reduced problem's pieces (after P0, `1b_p0.md`), an initial-value shot with one parameter, bit-identical to the
reviewer's independent integer mimic in all three cases and within 2e-16 of the exact equation at the two larger charges. It is not yet
a measured result: the two machines run the three cases in the eighth queue (the chains to match are 287ab3341297ee29, 1612ebff281e25d4
and 57a24cc16e160c1a). It is a matched approximation of the five-dimensional problem, as `PLAN.md` says: the deep AdS3 of the paper is
global with a finite circle, which changes ell at relative order w, about 3e-11 at the paper's charge; the leading term kappa_flat is exact.
