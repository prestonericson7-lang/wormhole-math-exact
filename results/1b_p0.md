# 1b, the first solution of the patched geometry: the x-independent problem (P0), 2026-11-12

**In plain words.** Maldacena and Milekhin's paper argues that the five-dimensional shape of its construction (its metric: the
description of how space and time are shaped) exists, and remarks that one could try to find it numerically in a more explicit form. This
page solves the simplest case of that problem (P0, where nothing changes along one direction, a circle) for three functions U, V and B
that describe the shape, on grids (the points where the equations are solved) of 128, 256 and 512 rows, in fixed-point integer arithmetic
(every calculation done with whole numbers in a set format, so any computer prints the same digits). No single grid is the answer: each
carries its own error, and at 128 rows the centre value (U at the grid's deep end) even lands on the wrong side of an exact reference
solution, so the result is the extrapolation of two grids to an infinitely fine one (Richardson extrapolation), and one grid alone should
never be quoted. That result, the centre value U_c = -0.594734016 from the corrected program, agrees to 1.03e-7 with the reviewer's
independent solution of the continuous equations, -0.594734119 (both COMPUTED on a PC; the reviewer is the independent AI session that
checks every program and page here). The two machines (two small ARM computers) ran the 128- and 256-row grids with identical output, using the
program as it was before review found a 64-bit sum in it that overflowed; those MEASURED runs are kept as the record of what the machines
printed; the machines then reran the fixed program on 11-12 (N = 128 at 10:28 AM, N = 256 at 11:04 AM) and printed the PC's corrected chains
09d5ac7eddb94cd4 and 7b497cb99b5103b9 line for line (MEASURED); and nothing here builds anything.

**First line:** this computes a geometry on paper; it does not create one. The fields below are the shape of the fifth dimension near the
brane of Maldacena and Milekhin's "humanly traversable wormhole" (arXiv:2008.06618) in the reduced two-dimensional problem the reviewer
derived from the five-dimensional Einstein-Maxwell equations, solved here in its simplest case, the one with no dependence on the brane's
circle direction. The N = 128 solution is MEASURED: the two machines ran the program (26,011 sweeps each, started 4:45:05 AM) and

## What was computed

The reviewer's reduced system (its stage 1 is on `1b_nogo.md`; the three equations are in the kernel's header, `code/wh_relaxt.c`) for the
three warp functions U, V and B of the metric, on rows uniform in t = ln(-y), where y is the conformal coordinate of the fifth dimension:
the cut, where the brane's data are given, at y = -1.94e-5 (t0 = -10.85, the reviewer's reconciled depth), and the deep end at y = -14
(t1 = ln 14), where the fields have reached their centre values to about e^-28 and the deviations defined below obey a zero Neumann
condition. The parameters are the reviewer's: Q2 = 6, the boundary S2 radius a = 0.02, L = 1, kappa = 1, in the frame where the circle
coordinate x has period 2 pi (so alpha = 2 pi a/L_x = a). The brane's data at the cut come from the Fefferman-Graham series in y (the
reviewer, 4:00 and 4:21 AM, with the log term's size at this cut checked by it): U0 = B0 = -t0 + v^2/(18 a^2) + (6 Q2 - 1)/(60 a^4) v^4 ln v
with v = -y, and V0 = ln a - t0 - 7 v^2/(36 a^2) - 3/2 of the same log term. The log term is -6e-12 here; the two free v^4 terms (the
energy and the pressure of the brane's state) are about 2e-12 to 3e-12 and are zero on this page until their values arrive.

The unknowns are deviations (u, v, b) from an exact reference solution of the same equations, the AdS3 x S2 throat with the same Q2: the
reference's w = 1/S0^2 is the root of 2 Q2 w^2 - 3 w - 12 = 0 (w = 1.132782218537, which hands Q2 = 6.000000000000 back to twelve
decimals), L3 = (3 + w/4)^(-1/2) = 0.551888855732 (ln L3 = -0.594408601290), V = ln S0 = -0.062338373461, U = ln(L3 coth e^t),
B = ln(L3 / sinh e^t). The reference's own discretization defect on the grid (the discrete operator applied to its node values, minus its
source) is computed in 128-bit arithmetic and subtracted, so that the zero deviation is the exact discrete solution of the reference
problem and what remains is the deviation's own second-order error. Everything is Q.60 fixed point with the row coordinate and the
reference's node values in Q.56 (t itself passes the Q.60 ceiling of 8 at this cut); the same bits on every machine.

## How it was solved

Red-black Gauss-Seidel over the rows, all three fields updated from one state, a fixed relaxation parameter 2 - 90/N (1.297 at N = 128,
1.648 at N = 256), the change guard armed and never triggered, the stops: the largest correction and the largest 128-bit residual both at
or below 2^-40. The data cannot be imposed at once: from any direct start (the deviations linear from the cut values to zero, or with the
V deviation given the shape the geometry has, (1/2) ln(1 + a^2 w e^-2t)) the corrections and the residual grew steadily at every
relaxation parameter tried (1.3, 1.0, 0.8, 0.5) and left the Q.60 range inside 63 to 128 sweeps, while a sixteenth and a quarter of the
same data converged in 2,760 to 5,136 sweeps. The scheme is stable near the reference and the direct start is outside its basin, as the
reviewer's reconciled design had warned ("start near the solution"). So the brane radius is ramped: ten stages with a doubling from
3.9e-5 to 0.02, which raises the V deviation at the cut by ln 2 a stage (0.76 to 7.00); the first stage starts from the profile above, each
later one from the previous stage's solution plus the profile's increment, and the small Fefferman-Graham terms enter only at the full
radius. Every stage converged: 2,559 to 2,789 sweeps at N = 128 (26,011 in all, 33 s on one PC core), 2,978 to 3,274 at N = 256 (30,608
in all, 60 s). The four columns of the grid (the data have no x dependence) agree to 1,189 units of 2^-60 at N = 128 and 19,714 at
N = 256 (1.7e-14), the stop tolerance's leftover.

## The result, after a correction from the review (7:45 AM)

point update the two x-neighbours were added in 64 bits before the 128-bit product. Where the V deviation is above 4 (the first 28 rows
at N = 128, nearest the cut) that sum passes the format's ceiling of 8 and wraps, which feeds V's equation a spurious term there; V and B
relax it away, U, which the equations see only through its derivatives, keeps it as a constant: U high at a = 0.02 by 3.63e-7 at
N = 128 and 3.45e-7 at N = 256, by 1.4e-5 at the larger brane of the alpha = 1/8 check, B by 8e-10, V by 2e-11. The exact-test models
could not see it (their deviations are zero or small). The fix is one product per neighbour. With it the kernel agrees with the
reviewer's exact solution of its form to every printed decimal at N = 128 and 256 (chains 09d5ac7eddb94cd4 and 7b497cb99b5103b9), and
the same wrap was removed from the one-field kernel

## What this is and is not

It is the first solution of the reduced problem's simplest case, with its error measured by grid doubling at N = 128, 256 and 512; with
the corrected kernel, its two-grid extrapolation agrees with the reviewer's independent continuum solution to 1.03e-7 in U_c (COMPUTED).
At N = 128 and 256 it is measured: the two machines printed identical lines with the PC's chains (46e8b5ae9ae59aa2 and
5a89a7f4c5f17234), from the build with the wrapped sum, and then from the fixed build (09d5ac7eddb94cd4 and 7b497cb99b5103b9, 10:28 and
11:04 AM on 11-12). The reviewer's exact solve of this kernel's own discrete equations is the direct
cross-check, and it found the wrapped sum of the correction above; the fixed build's reruns on the machines matched it to every printed decimal.
The central charge is printed
on the centre line; the charge integral Q_u follows when the reviewer's specification fixes its formula. The near-brane throat (P3) is
on `1b_p3.md`; the problem with dependence on the circle direction (P1, the mouths) is next.
