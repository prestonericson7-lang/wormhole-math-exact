# 1b, the first solution of the patched geometry: the x-independent problem (P0), 2026-11-12

**First line:** this computes a geometry on paper; it does not create one. The fields below are the shape of the fifth dimension near the
brane of Maldacena and Milekhin's "humanly traversable wormhole" (arXiv:2008.06618) in the reduced two-dimensional problem the reviewer
derived from the five-dimensional Einstein-Maxwell equations, solved here in its simplest case, the one with no dependence on the brane's
circle direction. The N = 128 solution is MEASURED: the two machines ran the same program through the night's queue (26,011 sweeps
each, started 4:45:05 AM) and printed 191 identical lines with the PC build's chain 46e8b5ae9ae59aa2; the PC that captures them restarted
for a Windows update meanwhile, and the machines, on their own firmware, finished and kept the output in their stores, read back at
6:12 AM (`results/1b_p0_board1.txt` is one machine's). The N = 256 and 512 solutions below are the PC build's, labelled, until the machines
reach them in the queue.

## What was computed

The reviewer's reduced system (its stage 1 is on `1b_nogo.md`; the three equations are in the kernel's header, `code/wh_relaxt.c`) for the
three warp functions U, V and B of the metric, on rows uniform in t = ln(-y), where y is the conformal coordinate of the fifth dimension:
the cut, where the brane's data are given, at y = -1.94e-5 (t0 = -10.85, the reviewer's reconciled depth), and the deep end at y = -14
(t1 = ln 14), where the fields have reached their centre values to about e^-28 and the deviations defined below obey a zero Neumann
condition. The parameters are the reviewer's: Q2 = 6, the boundary S2 radius a = 0.02, L = 1, kappa = 1, in the frame where the circle
coordinate x has period 2 pi (so alpha = 2 pi a/L_x = a). The brane's data at the cut come from the Fefferman-Graham series in y (the
reviewer, 4:00 and 4:21 AM, with the log term's size at this cut checked by it): U0 = B0 = -t0 + v^2/(18 a^2) + (6 Q2 - 1)/(60 a^4) v^4 ln v
with v = -y, and V0 = ln a - t0 - 7 v^2/(36 a^2) - 3/2 of the same log term. The log term is -6e-12 here; the two free v^4 terms (the
energy and the pressure of the brane's state) are about 1e-12 and are zero on this page until their values arrive.

The unknowns are deviations (u, v, b) from an exact reference solution of the same equations, the AdS3 x S2 throat with the same Q2: the
reference's w = 1/S0^2 is the root of 2 Q2 w^2 - 3 w - 12 = 0 (w = 1.132782218537, which hands Q2 = 6.000000000000 back to twelve
decimals), L3 = (3 + w/4)^(-1/2) = 0.551888855732 (ln L3 = -0.594408601290), V = ln S0 = -0.062338373461, U = ln(L3 coth e^t),
B = ln(L3 / sinh e^t). The reference's own discretization defect on the grid (the discrete operator applied to its node values, minus its
source) is computed in 128-bit arithmetic and subtracted, so that the zero deviation is the exact discrete solution of the reference
problem and what remains is the deviation's own second-order error. Everything is Q.60 fixed point with the row coordinate and the
reference's node values in Q.56 (t itself passes the Q.60 ceiling of 8 at this cut); the same bits on every machine.

## How it was solved

Red-black Gauss-Seidel over the rows, all three fields updated from one state, a fixed relaxation parameter 2 - 90/N (1.297 at N = 128,
1.648 at N = 256), the change guard armed and never triggered, the stops: the largest correction and the largest 128-bit residual both at
or below 2^-40. The data cannot be imposed at once: from any direct start (the deviations linear from the cut values to zero, or with the
V deviation given the shape the geometry has, (1/2) ln(1 + a^2 w e^-2t)) the corrections and the residual grew steadily at every
relaxation parameter tried (1.3, 1.0, 0.8, 0.5) and left the Q.60 range inside 63 to 128 sweeps, while a sixteenth and a quarter of the
same data converged in 2,760 to 5,136 sweeps. The scheme is stable near the reference and the direct start is outside its basin, as the
reviewer's reconciled design had warned ("start near the solution"). So the brane radius is ramped: ten stages with a doubling from
3.9e-5 to 0.02, which raises the V deviation at the cut by ln 2 a stage (0.76 to 7.00); the first stage starts from the profile above, each
later one from the previous stage's solution plus the profile's increment, and the small Fefferman-Graham terms enter only at the full
radius. Every stage converged: 2,559 to 2,789 sweeps at N = 128 (26,011 in all, 33 s on one PC core), 2,978 to 3,274 at N = 256 (30,608
in all, 60 s). The four columns of the grid (the data have no x dependence) agree to 1,189 units of 2^-60, the stop tolerance's leftover.

## What this is and is not

It is the first solution of the reduced problem's simplest case, with its error measured by grid doubling at N = 128, 256 and 512 and its
centre value agreeing to six digits with the reviewer's independent extrapolation. At N = 128 it is measured: the two machines printed
191 identical lines with the PC's chain. N = 256 waits in the queue (its chain to match is 5a89a7f4c5f17234), and the reviewer's own
defect-corrected run of the same scheme is the direct cross-check of the N = 128 centre value still to come (its first attempt died with
the PC's restart). The paper's
parameters from the solution (the effective c2, the charge integral Q_u) are printed once the reviewer's specification fixes their formulas.
The problem with dependence on the circle direction (P1, the mouths) is the next step; the near-brane throat (P3) is an ordinary
differential equation the reviewer has specified and verified, to be coded after this.
